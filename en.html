Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026.

1. Who is responsible

Data controller: WalletTracker. Contact: wallettrackercrypto@gmail.com. WalletTracker runs no servers of its own that process user data.

2. Data stored on your device

Public wallet addresses, balances, transactions, labels, settings and, if you import them, your exchange CSV files. Everything is stored encrypted (AES-256) on your device. We do not receive or copy it. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, it is lost (there is no cloud backup).

3. Data sent to third parties from your device

To show balances, transactions and prices, the app connects directly from your phone to public third-party services. Each service receives only what is needed for that request and, unavoidably, your IP address:

  • Block explorers and public nodes (for example Blockscout, Routescan, mempool.space, litecoinspace, Haskoin, BlockCypher, Koios, XRPL, TronGrid, Solana RPC, Horizon, Hyperliquid, PublicNode and other public nodes): receive the public address queried.
  • Price and exchange-rate services (Kraken, DefiLlama, Jupiter, Hyperliquid, Frankfurter): receive the coin or pair queried, not your addresses.
  • Etherscan, only if you add a key: receives that key and the public address queried.

Each service has its own privacy policy and is responsible for its own processing. We do not share your data with them beyond what is described. We do not sell data.

4. Purchases

The tax pass is bought through Google Play. Google processes the payment and the licence under its own policy; we do not receive your payment details. The app asks Google Play which purchases your Google account holds in order to unlock the matching tax years.

5. Feedback during testing

During the testing phase the app may open a Google Forms form in your browser so you can send feedback. It is voluntary: what you write there (and the app version, which is pre-filled) is received through Google Forms and processed by Google under its policy. Do not include keys, recovery phrases or addresses you do not want to share. This feature will not be in the final version.

6. This page

It uses no cookies, analytics or third-party resources. It is hosted on GitHub Pages: GitHub may log technical visit data (such as IP addresses) under the GitHub privacy statement. If you email us, we use your details only to reply.

7. Retention and rights

We keep no app data because we do not receive it. Support emails are kept only as long as needed to handle your request. You may exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability by writing to wallettrackercrypto@gmail.com, and lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Spain, the AEPD, aepd.es).

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 16.

9. Changes

If anything above changes we will update this page and its date.

WalletTracker is a tool to help you calculate and prepare your tax return; it is not tax advice. You are responsible for checking that your accounts, transactions and final figures are correct before filing. To the extent permitted by law, WalletTracker is not liable for errors, penalties or damages arising from the return you file.
